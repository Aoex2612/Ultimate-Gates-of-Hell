## What can you find here?

*   **Framedata:** Every move has it's own dedicated section on how they work, since a lot of them are pretty complex on their own right.
*   **Combo guides:** From the simplest, bread and butter combo routes to the most mechanically complex ones.
*   **Matchup guides:** An entire section dedicated to every matchup in the game: neutral, disadvantage, and advantage. **Including up-tilt back-air percentages!**
*   **Advanced tech and movement:** Every legal stage has it's separate section on how you can move around it, where to use dabk, how to stall, etc. Plus, you will learn on how to do every niche bayo tech, like footstool confirms, witch strikes, and more!

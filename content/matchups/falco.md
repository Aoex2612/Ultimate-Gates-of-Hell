# Overview

Falco is a winning matchup for Bayonetta. What makes it good is Falco's linear and reactable approaches, sub-par disadvantage and losing almost every air-to-air against abk. That being said, he can dish out a lot of damage with his combos and can kill Bayo easily if not careful; playing at a slow, comfortable pace and punishing his bad habits accordingly are key to beat him.

# Neutral

Since both Bayo and Falco have a **similar gameplan** (slow characters, with a highly focused bait and punish gameplan) **you can't just run into him**, as he'll punish you hard with his uptilts and fairs. Instead, **play second, let him approach** most of the time and only go in when the time is right.

### You SHOULD:
- **Put up your shield while he's in the air**, since none of his aerials are safe on shield and can be up b'd.
- **Space your aerials and abk his agression**, as you'll win most of these challenges and get tons of damage.
- **Jump oos and falling nair** **against his dtilt**.
- **Maintain yourself at mid range**, where he can't laser comfortably nor catch you with a shorthop fair. Also, because he's slow, you can dash away and jump away if he tries to get too close.
- **Approach with shorthops at full range** to dodge lasers.
- **Always be mindful of desperate side b approaches.**
- **Be aware of fair dragdown at 100%**, as dtilt/uptilt followups will start killing.

### You SHOULDN'T:
- **Scrap with him a ton**, he will generally win those and get a ton of damage.
- **Abuse abk at mid/full range** as his oos punishes are stong and can kill you with up smash on reaction.
- **Approach him aggresively while he backs up** since he can side b wiff punish your stuff or fair.

# Disadvantage

Falco's advantage is scary, so try to not find yourself on it in the first place, but you can still mitigate it when he puts you in his blender:
- **Di his up throw in.**
- **Di his landing fair up and away.**
- **Sdi his nair dragdown behind him.**
- Careful with **dtilt at the ledge.** It can even make you tech so press airdodge when getting hit by it just in case.

# Advantage

Falco's a fastfaller that gets hard comoboed by bayo, so make the most out of your damage. Some tips:
- **Uptilt bair kills around 100% at ledge**, and **130% center stage** (no rage nor staling factored in).
- **Fair 1-2** combos pretty well into **jab and uptilt**.
- **You can bair his side b trying to recover.**
- While ledgetraping, be mindful of **side b reversal attemps**. Shield them and punish, NEVER PARRY as he'll go away.

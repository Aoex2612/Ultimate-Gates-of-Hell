# Overview

Game&Watch is an even matchup for Bayo. Reasons are mostly because he's broken as a character, insane neutral, advantage and disadvantage. What makes it even for Bayo are her explosiveness with her combos, witch time and good disadvantage, which makes the matchup winnable. Still, GaW can be very hard to kill at times and have an unbreakable defense while being very punishing with his combos and ledgetrap, so patience and spacing are key in succeeding against him.

# Neutral

This is probably the hardest part of the matchup. He has tools for escaping uncomfortable situations and can beat most of your options.

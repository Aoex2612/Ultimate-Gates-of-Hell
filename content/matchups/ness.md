# Overview

Ness goes even versus Bayonetta. He's small and hard-hitting, with decent ledgetrap, incredible neutral, has a very potent backthrow that kills around 110-120% from center-stage and can put up good damage with the right combos. Bayo's biggest strength in this matchups is her overall better damage output per opening and advantage, since ness struggles to land, recover and get off the ledge.

# Neutral

This is the trickiest part of the matchup. Most of ness gameplan will reside on short hop fair, pk fire from platforms and dash dancing while being ready to wiff punish. Here's what you should and shouldn't be doing:

### You SHOULD:
- Stay on the ground when not attacking.
- **Space yourself with nair** and SOMETIMES fair (it's easy for ness to punish it from both sides with nair).
- Try to **dtilt his** grounded **approaches**.
- Use **dash shield a lot** to catch him short hop fair.
- **Grab him at random times** off of dash dances if he gets too comfy not pressing buttons and only shielding.
- Use **full hops as your panic button** since he's too slow in the air to catch you.
- **Up b oos bad approaches** with nair, fair and magnet.
- Try to **abk oos his spaced fair** once you get a hand on his timings.

### You SHOULDN'T:
- **Jump over him**. His oos game makes it impossible.
- **Abk at mid range**. It will wiff and his bair will punish you easily, or his upair if you land on a platform. He can also beat it with fair.

- **Bair too much**, since it's **easy for him to parry** due to it being too precise to land to mix-up your timings.
- **Abuse shield and rolling at around 120%**. You're due to getting back thrown or up smashed. Instead, use your movement to avoid him.
- **Fish for up tilt**. He has an easy time punishing you for being too greedy with it.

# Disadvantage

It's not too hard to avoid Ness's advantage, but keep in mind what he's looking for since you can get caught if not careful:
- He'll fish for up airs since it's lingering and beats every bayo hitbox from below. **Keep an eye on him and abk/dabk away or time your bats**.
- **Di and sdi away** all of his combos, specially pk fire and magnet conversions.
- **Mash up b if getting hit by his dtilt**, it true combos into itself so no point in shielding and he'll try to grab you, but up b will trade.
- Getting hit by pk thunder off stage is fine, so don't sweat avoiding it too much. Be **mindful of his fair (wich beats abk)**, his bair and upair and his yo-yo on the ledge. No need to mention pk flash as well.
- **Grab the ledge with abk** since it beats the charge hitbox from both up and down smash. **Mix-up your timings** so he doesn't catch you by releasing it.

# Advantage

Here's where you can win the matchup. Exploit it to the max and avoid dumb mistakes when edgeguarding him:
- He's **floaty** and **combo food**, so most of your stuff will connect.
- He's pretty **easy to hit with a witch strike**, so if the opportunity presents itself go for it.
- You can **frame trap** him well with upairs since his dair it's too slow to land with it consistently.
- He **struggles to get off the ledge** as his double jump is really bad for it and often gets mashy trying to challenge you and by rolling. **Shield and space yourself** a lot while ledgetraping him.
- His recovery is exploitable if you **keep an eye on where he's floating*. *Chase him and slowly push him away** until he's out of resources and either can't reach the ledge or you can get a witch time from his up b and finish him off.

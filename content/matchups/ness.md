# Overview

Ness goes even vs Bayonetta. He's small and hard-hitting, with decent ledgetrap, incredible neutral, has a very potent backthrow that kills around 110-120% from center-stage and can put up good damage with the right combos. Bayo's biggest strengh in this matchups is her overall better damage output per opening and advantage, since ness struggles to land, recover and get off the ledge.

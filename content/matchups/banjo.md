# Overview
Banjo's a winning matchup for Bayo. Main reasons for this are his bad physics for escaping combos, meaning he takes a lot of damage and can die from some combos if his sdi isn't the best. Plus his damage output is very low, specially compared to Bayo's, which makes it so that he has to win many more interactions than her in order to get the stock, even if he's heavy and hard to kill at times.
